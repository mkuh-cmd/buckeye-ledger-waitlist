Who this is for

Built for people who already read the box score.

This isn't another recruiting rumor mill or gameday hot-take feed — Eleven Warriors and On3 already do that better than we ever would. Buckeye Ledger is a stats product: automated, checked twice before it publishes, and built for the reader who wants the number, not the take.

That means it works for two kinds of people at once — the fan who wants a deeper look than the scoreboard gives, and the beat writer or producer who needs a fast, sourced number on deadline.
